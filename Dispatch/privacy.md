# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Dispatch ("we", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our web application.

## 1. Information We Collect
When you authorize our application via TikTok OAuth, we may access specific information provided by TikTok APIs based on the permissions you grant:
* **Basic Profile Info:** Public profile information (e.g., open_id, avatar, display name) to identify your session.
* **Video Performance Metrics:** Public performance statistics of your videos (e.g., views, likes, comments) via the TikTok Display API to generate your personal dashboard.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:
* To provide and maintain the core functionalities of the application (drafting, posting assistance, and data visualization).
* To improve user experience and troubleshoot technical issues.
* **We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data or TikTok metrics to any third parties.**

## 3. Data Storage and Security
* Your TikTok Access Tokens are encrypted and stored securely.
* We do not cache or store your TikTok video metrics long-term; data is fetched in real-time or retained only as long as necessary to display your dashboard.
* You can revoke our app's access at any time directly through your TikTok account security settings.

## 4. Compliance
Our application fully complies with TikTok's Developer Policies and standard data protection regulations. 

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository issues or jawgogogo@gmail.com.
